# Milestone 1 — Calidus to first platform

**Status:** Ready for execution — human decisions approved

**Acceptance difficulty:** Normal only

**Reference configuration:** `reference-mods-normal-factorio-2.1.24.lock.json`

## Goal

Deliver one small, playable story slice in Razi's existing Journal: orient a new
player on Calidus, carry the existing progression through the first
space-platform research and construction, and give the player an Archive
payoff when the first **real** space platform has been built. The slice must
remain useful when story text is unread, and its claims and timing must match
observable game state.

The proposed end-to-end span is the existing `first-signal` quest, the
`space-science` transition, the `platform` quest and platform project, and the
associated `corp-welcome` and `corp-departure` Archive records. These IDs and
the current progression graph are inputs, not permission to add research gates
or replace the Journal.

## Decisions required before execution

1. **Narrative canon — approved:** use the existing RAZI Industries /
   BLACKOUT-SEVEN material as canon for this slice. Workers may rely only on
   established material; they must not invent premise or plot facts.
2. **Narrative guidelines — approved:** use the spoiler-free rules in
   `Razi-protocol-3_situation.md`: concrete facts before evocative language,
   explain Razi-specific terms, keep objectives clear without optional lore,
   and make deeper reading optional.
3. **Archive timing — approved:** unlock `corp-departure` only after the
   existing force-owned `platform` project tracker observes a valid platform
   with a valid hub. Research alone, a pending platform, or a platform without
   a valid hub must not count. This deliberately changes its current
   research-based unlock.

The user approved the existing RAZI Industries / BLACKOUT-SEVEN canon, the
proposed clarity-first narrative guidelines, and the real-platform Archive
trigger on 2026-11-07. If implementation reveals a conflict with those
decisions, stop at `BLOCKED:` and revise this brief before proceeding.

## Interfaces and behavior contract

- Keep the existing IDs `first-signal`, `space-science`, `platform`,
  `corp-welcome`, and `corp-departure`.
- Treat research, platform construction, and completed voyages as distinct
  facts. This slice ends at construction; a maiden voyage is not implied.
- Use the existing `scripts/projects/platform_projects.lua` force-owned
  milestone as the authoritative construction evidence. Do not infer a build
  from research, a pending platform, or an invalid/missing hub.
- Preserve project recovery for an already-built valid platform, destruction
  and rebuild, save/reload, and force merges.
- The Archive reader and unlock notification must resolve the same authored
  record title. Do not add a one-off hard-coded title path.
- Story text must describe only current mechanics and tracked evidence.
  Essential next actions must remain understandable without reading the
  optional Archive record.

## Numbered success criteria

1. The release story switch remains off; story-enabled work is tested only in an
   isolated copy with the switch enabled before Factorio starts.
2. The selected slots for the two chapters, three quests, welcome, two records,
   and platform project are authored and use existing IDs. Extend the current
   slot checks to verify required slice slots are nonempty, within their
   documented length limits, and not duplicated; regenerate and validate
   `docs/story-slots.md`.
3. `corp-departure` stays unavailable after platform research alone and
   becomes available after a valid platform hub is observed. Repeated platform
   events do not duplicate the unlock or notification.
4. The same record title appears in the Archive list/reader and in its unlock
   notification; no story-specific title or record is exposed before its
   trigger.
5. The physical milestone remains correct after save/reload, valid-platform
   destruction/rebuild, a force merge, and a valid platform that existed before
   the Journal first queried its state.
6. Existing progression and unrelated projects retain their behavior. The
   added checks pass in Lua tests and in the Normal story-enabled FUnit /
   Factorio path using the pinned reference configuration.
7. A spoiler-free human checkpoint confirms the next action is clear without
   optional reading, the player retains ordinary factory freedom, and the
   reading burden and pacing are acceptable.
8. The completion report labels evidence using the repository's ladder:
   implemented, unit/Lua-tested, Factorio-tested, and human-accepted. Record
   remaining limitations and the work/time spent; do not claim campaign-wide
   acceptance.

## Execution procedure

1. The integrator inventories only gaps in this slice and records the existing
   targeted Lua and Normal FUnit baseline before changes. Trace the selected
   quest, project, Archive, and notification paths against current code; record
   any mechanic conflict here before work.
2. Freeze the slot IDs, title-resolution interface, required-content checks,
   and acceptance fixtures.
3. Assign the two disjoint lanes; keep the project/event-to-record interface
   owned by the integrator.
4. Run targeted Lua and localization checks, including negative cases before
   the build and positive cases after a valid hub exists.
5. Run the relevant story-enabled Normal FUnit / save-reload checks on the
   pinned pack in an isolated test copy. Do not alter the normal player
   installation or release switch.
6. Review only spoiler-free experience questions with the human; keep plot
   outlines, revealing excerpts, and story-specific screenshots out of
   progress reports.
7. Accept only when every criterion has evidence; otherwise return the
   responsible lane's bounded failures or mark `BLOCKED:`.

## Stop conditions and spoiler boundary

Stop and report `BLOCKED:` if the selected trigger cannot be established from
source-owned runtime evidence, the Archive and notification cannot share the
existing title-resolution mechanism, optional dependencies become necessary,
the story conflicts with current mechanics, or the approved canon is
insufficient to write the slice honestly. Escalate premise, mechanics, and
scope decisions rather than inventing answers.

Human-facing updates may name the systems and test evidence, but must not
include plot outlines, revealing excerpts, story-specific screenshots, or
future outcomes. Repository files and isolated worker workspaces may contain
the authored story.
